# Send crew or a contractor

**Status:** built 2026-11-09 (uncommitted; awaiting review)
**Owner:** Ong Zheng Kai

## Problem

When the app warns that equipment may be failing, the only next step it
offers is for the person reading the warning to judge it themselves. The asset
banner's "File an inspection" and the condition card's "Record assessment" both
ask whoever is looking to say whether a pump is faulty. A manager in the office
can't tell that, and shouldn't be asked to.

What really happens in an estate:

- **Something might be wrong** → the manager sends **crew** to look at it.
- **The crew find a fault** → the manager sends a **contractor** to fix it.
- **Equipment has reached the end of its life** → the manager sends a
  **contractor** to replace it, with no inspection first.

Today the app has no "send" step at all:

- A warning can't start an inspection for crew. The monthly check list has to
  be generated by hand, and crew are never told about it (Crew see no
  notifications).
- A warning can't start a work order. A work order starts only from a fault
  report or an inspection result, or from a blank form.
- A failed inspection doesn't tell the manager anything. They have to find it.

In this app "send a contractor" means **create a work order**. There is
no contractor directory; that is ID-37, which isn't built.

## Behaviour

### The app picks the route

- Every equipment warning shows **one** "send" button, chosen by the app from
  the reason for the warning. The manager does not choose between crew and
  contractor.

  | Why the equipment was flagged | Button |
  | --- | --- |
  | End of life (`FailureRiskFlagReason.END_OF_LIFE`) | **Send a contractor** |
  | Likely to break down (`FAILURE_RISK`) | **Send crew to inspect** |
  | Condition needs attention or faulty (`CONDITION`, or the attention banner) | **Send crew to inspect** |
  | Due for servicing (`SERVICE_DUE` notification) | **Send crew to inspect** |
  | Repair predicted (`REPAIR_PREDICTED` notification) | **Send crew to inspect** |

- When equipment is flagged for more than one reason, end of life wins:
  replacing it makes an inspection pointless.
- The buttons appear on the asset attention banner, on the asset forecast card,
  on matching notifications in the inbox, and on Due checks rows whose basis is
  "Predicted" with an end-of-life reason.
- The asset banner's "File an inspection" button and the condition card's
  "Record assessment" button are **not shown to readers who can send crew**.
  Crew still see Record inspection on the asset page, so they can do the job
  they were sent to do.
- "Sensor may not be working" is unchanged. It keeps Replace sensor (see
  plain-english-wording.md).

### Send crew to inspect

- Clicking **Send crew to inspect** opens a small dialog, "Who should inspect
  <equipment>?", with one required field: **Person**. It lists the estate's
  current members who can record inspections, by name with their role
  ("Ahmad Rahman · Crew"), sorted by name. "Can record inspections" uses the
  same server check that guards recording (today: owner, Manager, Crew), so a
  custom role with that permission is listed too. Buttons: Cancel and
  **Send** ("Sending…" while pending).
- Sending creates an `InspectionCheck` for the current Singapore month with
  `dueOn` = today, a new `dueBasis` `REQUESTED`, the manager as sender and the
  chosen person as the **assigned inspector**.
- If the equipment already has an open check this month, no new one is made.
  The button is replaced by "Sent to <person> · due <date>". Opening a
  check twice is never possible, even from two tabs at once.
- The new check appears on Due checks with the basis "Sent to <person> by
  <manager>".
- **Only the chosen person** gets a **"Please inspect <equipment>"**
  notification (new type `CHECK_REQUESTED`, addressed to them). It links to
  the equipment's Record inspection page. Other members don't see it.
- Anyone who can record inspections may still record it; assigning someone
  doesn't lock others out. Recording completes the check, the same way as for
  monthly checks (`completedByResultId`), and marks the `CHECK_REQUESTED`
  notification resolved.
- Success toast: "Sent to <person> to inspect <equipment>."

### Send a contractor

- Clicking **Send a contractor** opens the new work order form, already filled
  in:
  - **Source** "Maintenance recommendation";
  - the equipment, its location and its category;
  - **Reference note** "Reached end of life (expected <date>)";
  - **Description** "Replace <equipment>. It has reached the end of its
    expected life."
- The manager can edit any field, then saves as usual. Nothing is created
  until they save. Creating a work order commits money, so a person always
  confirms it.
- If the equipment already has a work order that isn't closed, the button is
  replaced by "Contractor already sent · <work order reference>", linking to
  it.

### When crew find a problem

- Recording an inspection as **Fail** requires a note: "What's wrong?"
  (1 to 2,000 characters). The form won't save a Fail without one ("Say
  what's wrong so the manager knows what to fix."), and the server returns 400
  if it's missing. A Pass keeps the note optional. Inspections already
  recorded are not affected.
- When an inspection is recorded as **Fail**, the estate's managers get a
  **"Crew found a problem with <equipment>"** notification (new type
  `CHECK_FAILED`). The reason line is the inspector's note, shortened.
- That notification's button is **Send a contractor**. It opens the work order
  form filled in from the inspection result, using the existing
  `sourceInspectionId` flow.
- If a work order already exists for that inspection result, the button is
  replaced by "Contractor already sent · <reference>".
- A **Pass** sends no notification.

### Who can press the buttons

- **Send crew to inspect:** the same people who can generate the inspection
  list today (the `POST /inspection-list/generate` check).
- **Send a contractor:** the same people who can create work orders today
  (owner, Manager).
- A reader who can see the warning but can't press its button sees "Ask
  <who> to send crew." or "Ask <who> to send a contractor." <who> comes from
  `who-can-help` (plain-english-wording.md) with two new actions,
  `SEND_CREW` and `SEND_CONTRACTOR`.

## Data

`packages/db/prisma/schema/schema.prisma`: add only. Each column is nullable or
gains a new value, so code that is already running is unaffected.

| Model | Change |
| --- | --- |
| `InspectionCheck` | New `dueBasis` value `REQUESTED` (fits the existing `VarChar(15)`). New nullable columns `assignedToUserId String? @db.VarChar(255)` and `assignedToName String? @db.VarChar(255)`, set only for `REQUESTED` checks. |
| `Notification` | New `type` values `CHECK_REQUESTED` and `CHECK_FAILED` (fit `VarChar(16)`). New nullable columns `inspectionResultId String? @db.Uuid` (set only for `CHECK_FAILED`, relation to `AssetInspectionResult`) and `recipientUserId String? @db.VarChar(255)` (set only for `CHECK_REQUESTED`). Index `[recipientUserId]`. |

One migration, expand-only. No contractor table. A null `recipientUserId`
keeps today's rule (the notification belongs to the estate, not a user).

## API

| Method | Path | Request | Response | Auth |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| POST | `/estates/{estateId}/assets/{assetId}/inspection-requests` | `InspectionRequest { assignedToUserId: string }` (required) | 201 `InspectionCheckResponse` (new check); 200 `InspectionCheckResponse` (the open check that already existed); 409 `EstateError` "Already inspected this month" (see edge cases); 400 assignee missing or can't record inspections in this estate; 404 asset not in estate | Same check as inspection-list generate; active estate and active asset |
| GET | `/estates/{estateId}/inspectors` | none | 200 `InspectorResponse[] { userId, name, roleName }`, sorted by name | Same as POST inspection-requests |
| GET | `/estates/{estateId}/assets/{assetId}/open-work-order` | none | 200 `OpenWorkOrderResponse { id, reference }`; 204 when none | Same as reading work orders |

Changed contract (regenerate with `pnpm api:generate`):

- `DueBasis` gains `REQUESTED`; `InspectionCheckResponse` gains nullable
  `requestedByName` and `assignedToName`.
- Creating an inspection result with `outcome` `FAIL` and blank `notes` returns
  400 (`notes` description updated).
- `NotificationType` gains `CHECK_REQUESTED` and `CHECK_FAILED`.
  `NotificationResponse` gains nullable `inspectionResultId`.
- `HelpAction` (who-can-help) gains `SEND_CREW` and `SEND_CONTRACTOR`.
- Notification visibility: a `CHECK_REQUESTED` notification is seen only by
  its `recipientUserId`, and only while that user can still record
  inspections in the estate. All other types keep today's rule (managers
  only).

The work order form takes two new optional URL parameters, `assetId` and
`reason=END_OF_LIFE`, to fill itself in. Saving uses the existing create
endpoint and the existing `MAINTENANCE_RECOMMENDATION` source.

## Edge cases

- **Already inspected this month.** `InspectionCheck` allows one check per
  asset per month. If this month's check is done, the button is disabled and
  shows "Inspected on <date> (<Pass/Fail>)". A Fail already offers Send a
  contractor. The API returns 409 if called anyway. See open question 2.
- **Two managers press Send crew at once.** One check is created and keeps
  the first manager's choice. The second call gets 200 with that check, and
  its dialog closes showing "Already sent to <person>".
- **Chosen person loses access before sending.** The server re-checks the
  assignee and returns 400. The dialog shows "<name> can no longer record
  inspections here. Choose someone else." and refreshes the list.
- **Assigned person leaves later.** The check stays open and on Due checks;
  their notification disappears (visibility is checked on read). The manager
  can see "Sent to <person>" and anyone else who can record may do it.
  Reassigning is out of scope.
- **Manager picks themselves.** Allowed. A notification to yourself is
  harmless.
- **Monthly list generated later.** Generating the list must skip equipment
  that already has a `REQUESTED` check this month (the unique constraint
  already enforces this). The list shows the requested check, not a duplicate.
- **Nobody else can record inspections.** The list always has at least the
  owner and Managers. If it has no Crew, the dialog says under the field:
  "No crew in this estate yet. Invite crew in People & access."
- **Inactive estate or retired equipment.** No send buttons; the API returns
  409 / 404 as other writes do.
- **Equipment flagged end of life but already being replaced.** The open work
  order check shows "Contractor already sent" instead of a second button.
- **A Fail recorded by a manager.** It still raises `CHECK_FAILED`. Other
  managers need to know, and it costs nothing.
- **Fail note.** Whitespace-only counts as blank. The limit stays at the
  existing 2,000 characters.

## Out of scope

- A contractor directory, choosing a company, or routing jobs to companies (ID-37).
- Reassigning a sent check, or assigning monthly-list checks to people.
- Creating work orders with no manager click, or opening checks for every
  flagged asset with no click.
- Changing how equipment gets flagged (failure-risk model, end-of-life
  dates, condition rules).
- Notifications by email or SMS.
- Generating the monthly check list automatically (it stays a button).

## Decisions

1. **Who goes:** the manager picks one person when sending crew. Sending is
   always a click, never automatic.
2. **One inspection per equipment per month** stays. After a Pass, the
   manager waits until next month.
3. **A Fail needs a note** saying what's wrong.
